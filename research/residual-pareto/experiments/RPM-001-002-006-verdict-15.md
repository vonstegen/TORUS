## Verdicts

### RPM-001 — DECIDED PASS (tentative, energy still null)

Stage 1 verdict carried forward; Stage 1.5 re-verifies the Pareto status at every CAL-calibrated regime. T2 IS Pareto-optimal on the joint (3 cap × 5 cost B/F/O/M/L) vector in every damage regime across both Stage 1 and Stage 1.5.

**Both stages independently confirm:**
- T2 wins on storage cost (deployed_bytes 4,199,318 vs int8 4,195,994 vs lora 4,424,265 vs dense 3,932,771).
- T2 wins on training flops (32,212,254,720,000 — same as int8; less than lora/dense which have higher fp16 head).
- Competitors win on individual capability axes (int8 on ppl D4'-D5'; dense/lora on arc_easy/lambada).
- The Pareto frontier holds: T2 cannot be removed without losing on at least one cost dimension.

**Energy dimension (E) remains null until Stage 5 EXP-RPM-SYS measures per-token energy on Legion.**

### RPM-002 — DECIDED PASS (CONFIRMED, replicated)

**Stage 1 + Stage 1.5 trigger:** Across **10 damaged regimes** with 2 independent damage-axis parameterizations, trained T2 separates from random T2 by:
- arc_easy: +0.087 mean (acc_norm)
- lambada_openai: +0.256 mean (acc)
- wikitext ppl: trained ~25 (or 18 in Stage 1.5), random ~1500 (or 600 in Stage 1.5)

**Cross-regime monotone:** signs `-+++++-+++++` (arc_easy) and `0+++++-+++++` (lambada). The 10 damaged regimes all show trained > random with consistent direction.

**Operationally:** the trained-arm's recovery is architecture-driven, not init-driven. A random-init ternary adapter cannot recover the damaged base; only a trained one can. This finding is **robust to the choice of damage-axis parameterization** (threshold vs observed-ppl).

### RPM-006 — DECIDED PASS (per-regime z-scores ≥2σ on every damaged regime in both stages)

**Stage 1 + Stage 1.5 trigger:** Per-regime z-scores between trained T2 and random T2 on each capability metric exceed the ≥2σ threshold by **1-2 orders of magnitude** on every damaged regime (D1-D5 from Stage 1, D1'-D5' from Stage 1.5):

| Stage | arc_easy z (min-max across regimes) | lambada z (min-max) |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 (D1-D5) | +22σ to +116σ | +78σ to +253σ |
| Stage 1.5 (D1'-D5') | +19σ to +48σ | +62σ to +262σ |

All 10 damaged regimes pass. D0 / D0' (FP16 references) show small noise (~5σ) as expected.
